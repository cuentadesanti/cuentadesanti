# Santiago Silva

Applied mathematician working across complexity science, computational social science, and spatial modelling. I am interested in how individual decisions, institutional rules, and spatial structure interact to produce unequal aggregate outcomes, especially in housing and territorial systems.

## Selected work

- **Territorial inequality as a measurement system** — Bayesian latent-variable and spatial analysis across 2,469 Mexican municipalities. The project treats official deprivation indices as distinct measurement systems rather than interchangeable ground truth. [Repository](https://github.com/cuentadesanti/pobreza-marginacion) · [Archived release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21344720)
- **Eating-disorder symptomatology among Mexican university students** — reproducible analysis of 610 students using six clinical instruments; published in the *International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health*. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph22121797)
- **Housing-market and cadastral valuation** — professional modelling of residential sale and rental markets across 20+ cities. The underlying data, code, and client deliverables are proprietary.

## Methods

Bayesian modelling and MCMC · causal inference and DAG-based research design · spatial statistics · predictive modelling · reproducible Python, R, and SQL workflows

I am open to research collaborations in complexity science, computational social science, and urban systems.
